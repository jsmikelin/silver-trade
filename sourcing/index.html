Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I choose a Hong Kong silver supplier over other Asian sources?

Hong Kong offers unmatched advantages: zero duties, transparent financial regulations, world-class logistics, and a long-established precious metals trading ecosystem. Combined with LBMA compliance and English/Japanese/Chinese-speaking account managers, Hong Kong suppliers provide a level of reliability and efficiency that other Asian sources often cannot match.

Do you ship to the United States and Japan?

Yes. We regularly export to US buyers through Los Angeles, New York, and Houston ports (15-25 business days) and to Japanese buyers through Tokyo, Yokohama, and Osaka ports (7-14 business days). We provide full customs documentation and import assistance for both markets.

What payment terms do you offer for wholesale silver orders?

Standard terms: 20% deposit via TT wire transfer to confirm the order, with the remaining 80% balance due within 10 days after price fixing and before month-end. For established trading relationships, flexible payment terms can be arranged upon review.

Can I visit your Hong Kong office or arrange an inspection?

Yes. We welcome buyer visits to our office at Unit 1618B, 16/F Pioneer Centre, 750 Nathan Road, Mong Kok, Kowloon, Hong Kong. Third-party inspections through SGS or CCIC can also be arranged for all orders.

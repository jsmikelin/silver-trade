Our 99.9% silver powder (200 mesh, approximately 74µm) serves the electronics and advanced materials industries — conductive pastes for circuit printing, front-side metallization for solar cells, contact materials, and powder metallurgy. Produced by atomization with a controlled particle size distribution, it delivers consistent sintering behavior and high electrical conductivity. Supplied in 25kg sealed drums with a certificate of analysis per lot, it is ideal for paste manufacturers, PV cell producers, and electronics component makers seeking a stable bulk supply chain. Applications: Electronics, Solar & Casting

Silver for Electronics

Silver's electrical and thermal conductivity makes it irreplaceable in conductive pastes for printed circuits, membrane switches, contacts, and EMI shielding. Our 200-mesh silver powder and 99.99% grains give component makers a consistent, certificate-backed raw material for paste formulation and alloying, with stable bulk pricing and documented lot traceability.

Silver for Solar

Photovoltaic cells rely on silver paste for front-side metallization — the fastest-growing industrial silver demand channel. Solar manufacturers need a dependable supply of high-purity silver powder with consistent particle size and sintering behavior. Our atomized 200-mesh powder is produced for exactly this use case, backed by per-lot certificates of analysis.

Silver Casting Grain

Jewelers and casting houses worldwide prefer 1–3mm silver grains for lost-wax casting and ingot production: uniform granule size means predictable melt behavior and fewer defects. Our 99.99% grains melt cleanly and consistently, and Japan's jewelry and metalcraft industries are among our most active casting-grain markets.

Quality Assurance & Compliance

Trust is the currency of the bullion trade, and every order we ship is backed by a documented verification chain. Silver authentication starts at the source: we source exclusively from LBMA-listed refiners and audit the refining certificates before any metal enters our stock. Each silver bar carries an individual serial number that is recorded on the commercial invoice, packing list, and third-party assay certificate — so your downstream buyer or customs broker can trace the metal from refiner to delivery. Silver grains and silver powder are verified by ICP-OES laboratory analysis, with the certificate of analysis issued per batch or lot. On request, we can arrange independent pre-shipment inspection (SGS/CCIC) at the Hong Kong vault before dispatch. This is the same verification discipline we explain in our guide to verifying LBMA compliance when sourcing silver bars — and it is how we protect buyers from the counterfeit and under-fineness risks that exist in the market.
